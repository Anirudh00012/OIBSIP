About Me

I am a passionate Product Designer and Full Stack Developer with over 14 years of experience creating innovative digital solutions. My expertise lies in transforming complex ideas into intuitive and visually appealing user experiences. I enjoy working on challenging projects that combine creativity, technology, and problem-solving. Throughout my career, I have collaborated with startups, agencies, and global businesses to design modern websites, web applications, and digital products. My goal is to create experiences that not only look beautiful but also deliver real value to users and businesses. I believe that successful products are built through a combination of thoughtful design, clean code, and continuous learning. When I'm not designing or developing, I enjoy exploring new technologies, mentoring aspiring developers, and staying updated with the latest industry trends.
